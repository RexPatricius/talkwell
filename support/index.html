Support

Having a problem or have a suggestion? Email support@talkwellapp.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Quick answers

How do I get a new question? Tap the screen or swipe right. Swipe left to go back to the previous question.

How do I switch to dark mode? Tap the appearance button in the top-right corner to cycle between System, Light and Dark.

How do I choose which questions I get? Tap the Decks button in the top-left corner and turn decks on or off. Each deck shows how many cards you have left. At least one deck stays on.

Will I see the same question twice? Not until you choose to. Talkwell remembers the cards you've seen, on your device only. To see them again, tap Start over at the bottom of the Decks sheet. When you finish every card in your chosen decks, you can also start those decks over from the last card.

Do I need an internet connection? No. Every question is built into the app, and new ones arrive with App Store updates.
