Talkwell

Questions worth asking.

Talkwell is a deck of conversation cards that lives in your pocket. Open it and there's one question on a clean screen. Tap or swipe for the next one, and swipe back to revisit the last.

No accounts, no feeds, no ads. Just good questions and the people you're with.

Hundreds of questions across five categories

  • Fun & Lighthearted: easy openers that get everyone laughing
  • Deep & Reflective: questions that go a little further
  • Hypotheticals: "what would you do if…" for curious minds
  • Memories & Nostalgia: stories you haven't told in years
  • Dreams & Ambitions: where you're headed and why

Simple by design

  • One question at a time
  • Light and dark appearance
  • Works completely offline
  • No sign-up, no tracking, no data collected
  • Supports VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, Dark Mode and Reduce Motion
