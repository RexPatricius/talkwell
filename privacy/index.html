Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026

Updated: October 12, 2026

Talkwell is a simple app that shows conversation-starter questions. It was built to work entirely on your device.

We don't collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no internet connection. All questions are built into the app, and new ones arrive through normal App Store updates.

What stays on your device. The app remembers two small settings: your choice of light, dark or system appearance, and whether you've dismissed the first-launch tip. They are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Third parties. The app contains no third-party code or services. Purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we don't receive your personal information from those purchases.

Children. The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.

If you email us. If you write to support@talkwellapp.com, we receive your email address and anything you put in your message. We use them only to reply to you and to fix problems you report. Our email is handled by Google, and we may use an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) to help read messages and draft replies; a person reviews every reply before it is sent. We don't sell or share your email, add you to a mailing list, or combine it with other data. We keep support emails for up to two years, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time by writing to the same address.

Contact. Questions about privacy: support@talkwellapp.com.

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation; we don't use them to identify or track visitors.
